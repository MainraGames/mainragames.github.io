Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for Mainra Games mobile applications and games

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Mainra Games ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared, and protected when you visit our website or use our mobile applications and games (collectively, "Apps").

Data practices can differ between Apps and may change as features are added. An App may have additional notices in its store listing or inside the App. This policy describes the practices that may apply; it does not mean every App collects every category of information listed below.

1.1 Website and contact requests

When you use our website's contact form, you may provide your name, email address, and message. The form is submitted to Formspree, which processes that information on our behalf or as an independent service provider under its own policies. If you contact us through WhatsApp, Discord, email, or another linked platform, that platform processes your information under its own privacy policy.

Our website also loads Font Awesome resources from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Your browser may send a request, including technical information such as your IP address, to that content delivery service. We do not intentionally use the website to collect precise location data, and the current website implementation does not use first-party cookies or local storage.

2. Information We May Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

Depending on the specific game or app, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information
  • Usage Data: App interactions, game progress, features used, session duration, crash reports
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, time zone settings, device settings
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not precise geolocation unless explicitly requested)

2.2 Information You Provide

We may collect information you voluntarily provide:

  • Account Information: Username, email address (if account creation is required)
  • Communications: Messages sent through in-app support or contact forms
  • User-Generated Content: Game statistics, achievements, custom content

2.3 Data Collection for Different Game Types

Offline Games: An offline App may store progress and preferences locally on your device. If it includes optional analytics, advertising, or online features, those features may transmit the related information when enabled or available.

Online Games: An App with online features may process multiplayer interactions, social features, game progress, and server communication logs as needed to provide those features.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To provide, maintain, and improve our Apps
  • Game Functionality: To enable game features, save progress, and provide personalized experiences
  • Analytics: To understand user behavior and improve our products
  • Technical Support: To provide customer support and troubleshoot issues
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and protect against harmful activities
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations

4. Third-Party Services

Some Apps may integrate with third-party services. Only services used by a particular App apply to that App, and those providers may process information under their own privacy policies:

4.1 Analytics and Crash Reporting

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: For usage analytics and app performance monitoring
  • Crashlytics: For crash reporting and app stability improvement

4.2 Advertising (if applicable)

  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements in free versions of our games
  • Unity Ads: Alternative advertising platform for some games

4.3 Game Development Platforms

  • Unity Technologies: Game engine analytics and performance data
  • Google Play Games Services: For achievements, leaderboards, and social features

4.4 Third-Party Privacy Policies

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these third-party services:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our Apps
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, legal process, or government request
  • Safety and Security: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Aggregated Data: Anonymized, aggregated data for research and analytics purposes

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services and maintain your game progress
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements
  • Improve our services through analytics

We will delete or anonymize your personal information when it is no longer needed for these purposes.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. The measures available to us depend on the App, service provider, and type of information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Access and Control

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal information
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request a copy of your personal information in a portable format
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing of your personal information
  • Objection: Object to processing of your personal information

8.2 Opt-Out Options

  • Analytics: You can limit analytics data collection through your device settings
  • Advertising: You can opt out of personalized advertising through device settings or ad preference controls
  • Push Notifications: You can disable push notifications through your device settings

9. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete such information from our systems.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws and provide appropriate safeguards for your personal information.

11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Apps may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

  • Store game preferences and settings
  • Remember user choices and game progress
  • Analyze app usage and performance
  • Provide personalized content and advertisements

You can manage these technologies through your device or platform settings. The website itself does not currently intentionally set first-party cookies or use local storage, although linked services and third-party resources may use their own technologies.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Notify users of significant changes through in-app notifications or other appropriate means
  • Provide the updated policy within our Apps

Your continued use of our Apps after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

13. Regional Privacy Rights

13.1 European Union (GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation, including the right to withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with supervisory authorities.

13.2 California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to know about personal information collection and the right to delete personal information.

13.3 Other Jurisdictions

We comply with applicable privacy laws in all jurisdictions where our Apps are available.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us:

  • Email: mainragames@gmail.com
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +62 851 1759 0001
  • Response Time: We aim to respond to privacy-related inquiries within 30 days.

15. Your Choices and Acceptance

Where applicable law requires consent, we will request it before collecting or using information for that purpose. You may withdraw consent or exercise other available rights by contacting us or using the controls provided by your device, platform, or the relevant App. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please stop using the relevant App or website.

Important: This policy is a general notice for Mainra Games. Specific Apps may collect less information or provide additional disclosures. Please review the privacy information presented for the specific App you use.
